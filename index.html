THE CHALLENGE

Land acquisition is not one process.
It is hundreds of disconnected processes.

A single infrastructure project involves ministries, states, districts, field officers, land records, compensation systems, physical documents and affected families. Decision-makers often cannot see the complete real-time status in one connected view.

📁

Fragmented Records

Project documents, land records and acquisition status can exist across different departments and systems.

🗺️

Disconnected Geography

A report may tell you a parcel is delayed, but decision-makers may not immediately see where that parcel is located.

⏳

Late Problem Detection

Issues are often discovered after delays have already affected the project timeline.
