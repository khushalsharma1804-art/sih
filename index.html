01 • The Problem

Land acquisition data is fragmented.

Critical infrastructure projects involve multiple ministries, states, districts, authorities and landowners. Information is often distributed across disconnected systems, documents and manual workflows.

01

Fragmented Systems

Project, parcel, compensation and possession information exists across multiple agencies.

02

Limited Visibility

Decision makers cannot easily see the live status of every project and affected parcel.

03

Delayed Resolution

Issues can remain unresolved because responsibility and escalation are unclear.

04

Reactive Decisions

Risks, delays and anomalies are often noticed after they already impact the project timeline.

02 • The Solution

One national platform.
One source of truth.

BHUMI-CHAIN connects spatial information, parcel-level digital twins, acquisition workflows, compensation, possession, AI intelligence and decision support into one unified platform.

Project Create or import government project data
→
GIS Identify land boundaries and affected parcels
→
Digital Twin Create one live record for every parcel
→
AI Intelligence Detect risk, delays and anomalies
→
Action Route tasks and escalate unresolved issues

04 • System Architecture

One connected
governance
ecosystem.

As the user scrolls, each layer of BHUMI-CHAIN becomes visible. Every layer contributes to one continuously updated national source of truth.

01 / SPATIAL LAYER

2D GIS Intelligence

Government project boundaries, corridors and affected land parcels are visualized and geographically connected to the acquisition lifecycle.

02 / VISUALIZATION LAYER

Cesium 3D Digital Twin

Imported spatial information is transformed into an interactive 3D environment for project-level and parcel-level visualization.

03 / DATA LAYER

Parcel Digital Twin

Each parcel becomes a live digital entity containing ownership, acquisition, compensation, possession and verification data.

04 / PROCESS LAYER

Acquisition Workflow Engine

The platform tracks each stage from proposal and verification to award, compensation, rehabilitation and possession.

05 / INTELLIGENCE LAYER

AI Decision Support

Deterministic intelligence identifies risk factors, compensation anomalies, delayed workflows and recommended actions.

06 • Automated Governance

Detect → Assign → Track → Escalate.

Once a high-risk issue is identified, BHUMI-CHAIN transforms intelligence into accountable action.

01

Risk Detected

AI intelligence identifies a compensation, workflow or acquisition issue.

02

Task Created

The system creates a structured task linked to the affected parcel.

03

Officer Assigned

Responsibility is routed to the appropriate authority based on role and jurisdiction.

04

SLA Monitoring

Deadlines and pending actions are continuously tracked for accountability.

05

Escalation

Unresolved issues can be escalated through district, state and central levels.
