THE PROBLEM & OUR SOLUTION

Land Acquisition Is a Coordination Problem

Multiple authorities, fragmented records and disconnected workflows make it difficult to monitor the real status of every land parcel infrastructure projects depend on.

📁

Fragmented Data

Project, land and financial data exist across disconnected systems.

⏳

Delayed Workflow

Manual follow-ups make bottlenecks difficult to detect early.

⚠️

Hidden Risks

Disputes and pending compensation become visible too late.

📊

Limited Visibility

Decision-makers receive reports instead of live operational context.

OUR CORE IDEA

A Live Operational Digital Twin

BHUMI-CHAIN gives every land parcel a digital identity that connects its geographic location, acquisition stage, compensation, possession, documents, workflow and risk.

END-TO-END WORKFLOW

How BHUMI-CHAIN Works

From an existing government infrastructure project to parcel-level monitoring and decision support.

1

Project Creation

Government agency creates the infrastructure project.

2

Spatial Import

Import existing GIS, GeoJSON, cadastral or project data.

3

Parcel Identification

Project area is linked with individual land parcels.

4

Digital Twin

Every parcel receives a complete live digital profile.

5

Workflow Tracking

Verification, notification, award, compensation and possession.

6

AI Intelligence

Risk, delay, anomaly and recommendation generation.

NO MANUAL REDRAWING

Government projects already have plans. BHUMI-CHAIN integrates them.

The system is designed around importing existing spatial and project data rather than forcing officers to manually redraw infrastructure boundaries.
